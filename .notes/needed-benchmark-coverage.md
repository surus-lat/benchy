  1. Indigenous languages — Quechua (several varieties), Guaraní, Aymara, Nahuatl, Mapudungun,
  Wayuunaiki, Bribri, Wixarika, etc. FLEURS has none of these.
  2. Regional Spanish varieties / accents — Mexican, Rioplatense, Andean, Caribbean, Chilean. FLEURS
   lumps them all under es_419 with a small sample of speakers.
  3. Spontaneous / non-read speech — FLEURS is read-aloud studio audio. Real LATAM use cases (call
  centers, podcasts, voice notes, WhatsApp audio) are spontaneous with noise, code-switching, and
  disfluencies.
